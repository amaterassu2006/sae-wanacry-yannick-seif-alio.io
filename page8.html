How Did the World React to WannaCry?

  • Hospitals and businesses experienced significant stress, with employees forced to stop using their systems.
  • Several national cybersecurity agencies released guidelines to better protect against this type of ransomware.
  • Some companies adopted stricter cybersecurity policies, including frequent backups, more regular updates, and security audits.

Change in standards:

  • The MS17-010 vulnerability forced companies to adopt better patch management practices.
  • Security standards evolved to include stricter requirements for backups, network segmentation, and vulnerability management.
  • Cybersecurity policies integrated early detection strategies and rapid response in case of compromise.
