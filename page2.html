The Origins of the WannaCry Malware

According to the FBI's Cyber Behavioral Analysis Center, the ransomware's language files were configured with Hangul fonts, suggesting that the computers were set to the UTC+09:00 time zone, which is used in Korea. Additionally, similarities between the code of WannaCry and malware used by the North Korean group Lazarus, which was involved in the 2014 cyberattack on Sony Pictures, were detected by researchers from Google, Microsoft, Kaspersky Lab, and Symantec. On December 18, 2017, the U.S. government officially accused North Korea of being behind the WannaCry cyberattack.
