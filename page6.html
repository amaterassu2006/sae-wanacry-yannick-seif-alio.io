Who Were the Victims of WannaCry?

The first infections occurred in Spain and the United Kingdom before spreading worldwide within a few hours. On the first night, around 100,000 Windows systems were infected. The main victims included:

  • Over 230,000 Windows PCs in 150 countries.
  • The most affected countries were Russia, Ukraine, India, and Taiwan.
  • The UK's National Health Service (NHS) was severely impacted, with nearly 70,000 devices infected, leading to the cancellation of surgeries.
  • Other major affected companies include Telefónica, FedEx, Nissan, Hitachi, and Renault.
  • Organizations such as the Russian Ministry of Interior, Andhra Pradesh Police in India, and universities in China were also affected.
  • WannaCry caused significant financial losses, estimated at around 4 billion dollars.
