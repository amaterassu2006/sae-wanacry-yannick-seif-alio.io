Patching WannaCry

The day after the attack, Microsoft released security updates to patch the vulnerability exploited by WannaCry. On March 14, 2017, a security patch was released, but the initial attack continued. On May 19, 2017, hackers attempted to launch a DDoS attack to take down the WannaCry domain, but it was protected by May 22, 2017. Free decryption tools are now available, and security experts have developed software like Wanakiwi to recover locked data.

  • The spread was stopped by a cybersecurity researcher, Marcus Hutchins, who discovered a "kill switch" in the source code, which helped limit the malware's impact.
  • Researchers from University College London and Boston University reported that their PayBreak system could defeat WannaCry.
