How Did WannaCry Spread?

WannaCry spreads automatically through a transport mechanism without any action from the victim. The code scans networks for systems vulnerable to EternalBlue, installs DoublePulsar (a backdoor), and then executes a copy of itself. Unlike other ransomware that relies on phishing or social engineering, WannaCry could also exploit existing DoublePulsar infections.
