What is WannaCry Malware?

WannaCry is a crypto-ransomware, a type of malicious software used by cybercriminals to extort money. It holds a victim's data hostage and promises to restore it in exchange for a ransom paid in cryptocurrency (Bitcoin). If the ransom is not paid, the data remains encrypted or may be deleted.

WannaCry is an example of crypto-ransomware. It holds data hostage and promises to restore it after receiving a ransom in cryptocurrency (Bitcoin).
