Windows Vulnerabilities

The spread of WannaCry was enabled by the zero-day exploit EternalBlue. Developed by the NSA as part of its program to store and exploit cybersecurity vulnerabilities, EternalBlue was stolen by the hacker group Shadow Brokers, who released the exploit on April 14, 2017. EternalBlue targets Windows operating systems. Petya is another ransomware that used the EternalBlue code.

  • EternalBlue connects to exposed SMB ports, which should never be open to the internet. A data center operator must ensure that ports 135-139 and 445 are not publicly exposed.
